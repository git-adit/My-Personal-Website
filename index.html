Gerald the Giraffe

Gerald is a tall and friendly giraffe who loves eating leaves and exploring the African savanna.

My Hobbies

  • Eating acacia leaves
  • Walking around the savanna
  • Hanging out with other giraffes

Where I Live

Gerald lives in the African savanna. The savanna has open grasslands, tall trees, and many amazing animals.

Interesting Facts

Giraffes are the tallest land animals in the world. Their long necks help them reach leaves at the tops of trees.
